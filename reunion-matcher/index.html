About this app

A little party helper for the CREST '76 yearbook (John High School). Everything runs on your own phone — no photo ever leaves your device.

🎵 "I'd like to teach the world to sing…"

How it works

  1. Set up — load your yearbook pages. The app finds the faces and reads the names beneath them. You check them and save.
  2. Reunion — point at someone, take a photo, and the app shows the three most likely classmates with a percentage.

Honest about the guess 🎲

Comparing a photo from today with a grainy black-and-white shot from nearly fifty years ago is genuinely hard. So treat the percentage as a playful hint, not the truth. That's why the app always shows the top 3 — the right one is often in there.

Tip: the sharper and more head-on the photo, the better the guess.

Back up your classmates 💾

Everything is stored on this phone only. Before you travel, tap Back up on the Set up screen to save a copy (to your email, Files, or cloud). If anything ever goes wrong — phone wiped, app reinstalled, even a different phone — tap Restore and pick that file to get everyone back. No yearbook or internet needed.
